# 2026 XHS Title/Cover Playbook (Execution Reference)

## Core Direction

- 2026 style: stronger hooks, but avoid pure clickbait.
- Prioritize: `relevance + deliverable promise + consistency with body`.
- Keep title short (prefer <= 20 Chinese chars for high-density scenarios).

## Reusable Copy Skills

1. SVO pattern (主谓宾): 人群 + 动作 + 结果.
2. Keyword first 6-10 chars for search intent.
3. Contrarian angle with proof path: 误区 -> 结论 -> 可执行动作.
4. Problem-solution format for saves/收藏.
5. Carousel promise format: `10张讲清X，避开Y`.
6. Cover three-line hook (pain -> method -> outcome).
7. Dual meaning title: hook layer + info layer.
8. Light anxiety only; avoid fear manipulation.
9. Pain points + method + bounded guarantee (no absolute claims).

## Cover Engineering

- Main title visually 2-3x subtitle.
- One focal idea per card.
- Keep visual anchor consistent (icon/avatar marker).
- White space is intentional; avoid overcrowding.

## Card Writing Constraints

- One card, one main claim.
- Prefer 3-5 bullets per card.
- Replace broad adjectives with concrete action/steps.
- CTA should ask for one immediate action.

## Compliance Guardrails

- Avoid absolute claims: "100%", "唯一", "最强", "永久".
- Avoid unverifiable personal branding claims.
- Keep title promise consistent with body delivery.
